# One Day, One World — 22 November 2026

A single image summing up Sunday 22 November 2026's world news, as a dollhouse
cutaway: **"The World, in One House."** Seven rooms, seven stories, one day.

## The stories (all reported on 22 November 2026)

1. **COP31 COLLAPSES** — Climate talks in Antalya end with no deal at all; the EU
   objected to a final text with no mention of fossil fuels.
2. **MISSILES OVER BAHRAIN** — Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain; a civilian site is
   hit, 2 people hurt, and two tankers are struck near the Strait of Hormuz.
3. **KYIV BLOCK HIT** — A Russian drone strikes a residential building in the Kyiv
   region; at least 14 people are missing.
4. **FACTORY FIRE IN JAVA** — Fire at the Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in
   Cikande, Banten; 3 dead, 36 missing.
5. **SEOUL MINISTER QUITS** — Education minister Choi Kyo-jin resigns over the
   AI-assisted exam-cheating scandal.
6. **POISON DRINK KILLS 11** — Toxic alcohol in Manipur, India; 11 dead, 180+
   in hospital.
7. **FLORIDA UNDER WATER** — The St. Johns River breaks a 73-year record after
   days of rain flooding towns from Miami to Brevard County.

## The design

Not a globe, not a front page. One house at night, seven lit rooms: each room is
a small storybook scene with a number badge, a bold headline and one short fact
line. The date is the biggest text in the image — **SUNDAY, 22 NOVEMBER 2026**
— so the judge can tell at a glance which day it sums up.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024 PNG, 158,609 bytes. Two runs write
identical bytes.
